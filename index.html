Our Mission & Vision

Have Grove Community Society is a registered non-profit society in British Columbia, dedicated to promoting community living and social inclusion for individuals with diverse abilities and support needs.

We believe every person deserves the right to participate fully in community life — to live where they choose, build meaningful relationships, and contribute their unique gifts to the world around them.

Guided by the principles of respect, dignity, and self-determination, we work alongside individuals, families, and communities to create a more inclusive British Columbia.

See Our Programs

Our Core Values

  • 🌱 Dignity & Respect — Every person is valued, heard, and treated with honour.
  • 🤝 Inclusion — We remove barriers so everyone can fully participate.
  • 💡 Self-Determination — People have the right to direct their own lives.
  • 🏘️ Community — Connection and belonging are fundamental human needs.
  • ⚖️ Equity — We advocate for fairness and equal opportunity for all.
  • 🌿 Sustainability — Building lasting supports that endure for generations.

Our Commitment

Community Living & True Inclusion

Community living is more than a place to live — it is about being a full and valued member of your neighbourhood, town, and province. Have Grove Community Society works to make that vision a reality for every person we serve.

We are guided by the values of the Community Living BC (CLBC) framework and the United Nations Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities — recognizing that inclusion is not a privilege, but a right.

Our approach is rooted in relationships, partnerships with families, and collaboration with local governments and community organizations across British Columbia.

About Have Grove Community Society

Have Grove Community Society is incorporated under the Societies Act of British Columbia. We are a member-led, non-profit organization governed by an elected Board of Directors committed to our mission of community living and inclusion.

Our society was founded with the belief that people with diverse abilities deserve the same opportunities, rights, and supports as any other member of the community. We are guided by our bylaws, which reflect our commitment to democratic governance, fiscal responsibility, and member participation.

We collaborate with Community Living BC (CLBC), the Ministry of Social Development and Poverty Reduction, and a wide network of community partners to deliver our services across BC.
